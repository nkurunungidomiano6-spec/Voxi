How It Works▾

Voxi pays 10% profit daily on your active deposited capital, for 50 days — a total return of 500% over the full cycle. Once your 50-day cycle completes, you can re-invest your balance to start a new cycle and keep earning.

How to Deposit▾

Go to "Add Money" from your dashboard, enter your mobile money phone number and the amount you want to invest, then tap Invest Now. Your deposit is confirmed automatically once payment goes through, and your 50-day earning cycle begins right away.

How to Withdraw▾

Go to "Withdraw" from your dashboard, enter the amount you want to cash out, then tap Withdraw. Funds are sent automatically to your registered mobile money number — no manual approval needed.

How to Refer Friends▾

Share your personal invite link from the Referrals page with friends and family. When they sign up and invest, you automatically earn a commission on their deposit — plus smaller commissions on your 2nd and 3rd level network too.
